Client information

Get Food

What to expect, what to bring, and when to visit the CAP Food Pantry.

Your first visit

Bring a photo ID with your current address.

If your ID does not show your current address, please bring an official piece of mail showing your current address. Your address must be within CAP’s serving area.

What to expect

How the food pickup process works

1

Sign in

All clients sign in when they enter the pantry. New clients receive a registration form requesting general information such as name, address, phone number, and household members.

2

Complete registration

When your name is called, the CAP Food Manager reviews your registration form and provides information about CAP.

3

Receive your food

Once registered, you return to the waiting area and your bags of food are carried out to your car.

Return visits

What to bring after your first visit

For return visits, clients must provide:

  • A photo identification card such as a driver’s license or state-issued ID OR proof of an address in CAP’s service area.
  • A self-declaration of need.

Examples of self-declaration of need

  • Independence Card (food stamps)
  • Social Services referral
  • CAP church referral
  • State Medical Assistance card
  • Termination, unemployment, or layoff
  • Residence at Friendship Station or Friendship Village, Stoney Hill, a local motel, or similar housing

Service area

Who CAP serves

CAP serves residents of Gambrills, Odenton, Severn, Fort Meade, Hanover, and Harmans, as well as the Anne Arundel County portions of Jessup and Laurel.

Your photo ID or official mail should show an address within this service area.

Food assistance

How often can I receive food?

Every 4 weeks

You may pick up food every 4 weeks. Food generally lasts about 5–7 days.

Emergency assistance

Emergency requests for assistance sooner than 4 weeks are handled on a case-by-case basis.

What you receive

What food can I expect?

🥫

Pantry food

Food typically includes canned tuna, chicken, beef stew, green beans, potatoes, tomatoes, soup, fruit, frozen meat, peanut butter, jelly, macaroni and cheese, pasta, pasta sauce, juice, cereal, and assorted bakery items.

🛒

Grocery voucher

Clients also receive a voucher to a local grocery store for items such as:

  • Bread
  • Milk, butter, cheese, yogurt, and other dairy case items
  • Fresh or frozen fruits and vegetables
Holiday Sharing: CAP participates in Holiday Sharing for Thanksgiving and Christmas. Registration begins October 1 and runs through December. You must be a current CAP client to participate.
