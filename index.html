Food assistance in the Odenton area

Food and support for our neighbors.

The Christian Assistance Program (CAP) Food Pantry helps local individuals and families challenged by hunger and food insecurity, regardless of religious belief or affiliation.

Serving: Gambrills, Odenton, Severn, Fort Meade, Hanover, Harmans, and the Anne Arundel County portions of Jessup and Laurel.

Holiday Sharing: Registration for Thanksgiving and Christmas food begins October 1 and runs through December. You must be a current CAP client to participate.
Weather & holiday closures: CAP follows the Anne Arundel County school system for weather-related closures. If schools are closed, the pantry is closed. If schools open late on Tuesday, the pantry is closed. If schools close early on Thursday, the pantry is closed. CAP is also closed on Juneteenth, July 4th, Thanksgiving week, Christmas week, and New Year’s Day.

About CAP

More than 40 years of community support.

CAP was formally established in 1985 as a cooperative ministry when several churches joined forces to better meet the needs of the community.

Today, area churches, local businesses, and individuals continue to work together to help residents facing hunger and food insecurity.
