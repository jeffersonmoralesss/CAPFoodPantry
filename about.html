About CAP

Churches, businesses, and individuals working together to meet the needs of local residents challenged by hunger and food insecurity.

Who we are

Christian in identity. Open to neighbors in need.

The Christian Assistance Program (CAP) Food Pantry is comprised of area churches, local businesses, and individuals that come together to meet the needs of local residents challenged by hunger and food insecurity.

Although CAP is a Christian organization, food is provided to qualified individuals and families regardless of religious belief or affiliation.

Service area

Serving our local community

Gambrills, Odenton, Severn, Fort Meade, Hanover, Harmans, and the Anne Arundel County portions of Jessup and Laurel.

Since 1985

More than 40 years of service.

CAP was formally established as a cooperative ministry in 1985, when several churches joined forces to better meet the needs of the community.

CAP is supported by the Anne Arundel County Food Bank, as well as churches, businesses, and individuals in the local area dedicated to helping those in need.
